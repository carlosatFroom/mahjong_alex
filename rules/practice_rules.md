# American Mahjong — practice table rules summary

This is the rule set the practice table enforces. Alex should review it before launch.

## Tiles (152)
- Three suits, numbers 1–9, four of each: Bams (Green Dragon's suit), Craks (Red Dragon's
  suit), Dots (Soap / White Dragon's suit).
- Winds: North, East, West, South, four each. Dragons: Green, Red, Soap, four each.
  Soap also stands for zero in year hands (2026 = 2, Soap, 2, 6).
- 8 Flowers (all interchangeable) and 8 Jokers.

## Deal
East (the dealer) gets 14 tiles, everyone else 13. Seats are East, South, West, North,
and play moves to the right (East → South → West → North).

## The Charleston
1. First Charleston (required): pass 3 tiles right, then 3 across, then 3 left.
2. On the last pass (left) you may pass blind: forward 1–3 of the tiles you are receiving
   without looking at them, plus enough of your own to make 3.
3. Second Charleston (optional): if every player agrees, pass left, across, right. Any
   player may stop it. Blind passing is allowed on its last pass (right).
4. Courtesy pass (optional): you and the player across agree on 0–3 tiles to swap; the
   smaller offer wins.
5. Jokers can never be passed.

## Play
- East discards first. Then each player in turn draws one tile from the wall, may swap
  jokers (below), and discards one tile, or declares Mahjong.
- **Calling**: when a tile is discarded, any player may claim it to complete an exposure
  of 3 or more identical tiles (pung 3, kong 4, quint 5, sextet 6) and place the group face
  up. The caller then discards, and play continues to their right. A discard may be
  claimed for a single or pair only to complete Mahjong.
- **Priority**: a Mahjong claim beats an exposure claim. Between equal claims, the player
  next in turn after the discarder wins.
- After calling, the caller must expose the group at once. Exposures cannot be changed.

## Jokers
- A joker can stand in for any tile in a group of 3 or more identical tiles (pung, kong,
  quint, sextet). Never in a single or a pair.
- A discarded joker is dead: nobody may call it.
- **Joker swap**: on your turn, if any player's exposure contains a joker and you hold the
  real tile it stands for, you may trade your tile for that joker.

## Winning
- You win (Mahjong) when your 14 tiles exactly match one hand on the card. Hands marked
  **C** (concealed) must be completed without exposures, except the final tile, which may
  be claimed from a discard. Hands marked **X** may use exposures.
- "Any 3 suits" means three different suits; "matching Dragons" means the Dragon of the
  same suit; consecutive numbers must be in order with no gaps.
- **Dead hand**: if your exposures can no longer form any hand on the card, your hand is
  dead. You keep playing and discarding but cannot win this game.
- If the wall runs out with no winner, it is a **wall game**: nobody scores.

## Scoring
- The winner collects the hand's card value from each player.
- Won on a discard: the discarder pays double, the others pay single.
- Self-drawn (winning tile from the wall): every player pays double.
- No jokers in the winning hand doubles all payments again, except Singles and Pairs
  hands, which can never use jokers.

## The practice card
The table uses an original practice card with the same categories as a real card: year
hands (2026), 2468, Like Numbers, Quints, Consecutive Runs, 13579, Winds and Dragons, 369,
and Singles and Pairs. Hand values range from 25 to 50.
