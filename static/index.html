    How this table plays

    • Charleston: pass 3 right, across, left. Then everyone may agree to a second Charleston (left, across, right). The last pass of each may be blind. Finish with an optional courtesy pass across.
    • Turns: draw, then discard. East starts with 14 tiles and discards first.
    • Calling: take a discard to expose a pung, kong or quint, or to win. Mahjong beats an exposure; otherwise the next player in turn wins the tile.
    • Jokers: only in groups of 3 or more, never in singles or pairs, never passed. A discarded joker is dead. On your turn you may swap a real tile for a joker in any exposure.
    • Winning: your 14 tiles must match one hand on the card exactly. Concealed (C) hands allow no exposures.
    • Dead hand: if your exposures fit no hand on the card, you play on but cannot win.
    • Scoring: discarder pays double; self-drawn, everyone pays double; jokerless doubles again (except Singles and Pairs).
